Privacy Policy

Last updated August 19, 2026

Karmine ("we," "us," "our") operates the Karmine platform at karmine.us, a peer-to-peer service that matches Clash Royale players for real-money 1v1 wagers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it — including phone numbers used for our SMS match-notification feature — and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and password (stored hashed, never in plain text).
  • Game information: your Clash Royale player tag and Supercell ID friend link.
  • Contact information: your phone number, collected only to send match-notification SMS texts (see Section 3).
  • Financial and wager information: wallet balance, deposit and withdrawal records, wager amounts, and match outcomes.
  • Technical information: standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) used for security and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate matchmaking and process wagers.
  • To process deposits, withdrawals, and settle wager outcomes.
  • To send transactional SMS notifications when you're matched with an opponent (see Section 3).
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse.
  • To respond to support requests.

3. SMS / Phone Number Use

If you register for a Karmine account and provide a phone number, we use it for one purpose: to send you a text message when you're matched with an opponent, containing their Clash Royale friend link so you can add them and play.

Frequency: you'll only receive a text when you're matched — frequency varies based on how often you queue and get matched. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or reply HELP for help.

We do not use your phone number for marketing purposes, and we never sell your phone number, or any other personal information, to third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, including your phone number, to any third party. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform under confidentiality obligations — for example, our SMS delivery provider (Twilio), our payment processor, and our hosting provider — solely to perform services on our behalf.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law (e.g., in response to a subpoena).
  • Parties as necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Karmine or our users, such as during a fraud investigation.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and transaction data for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal and financial recordkeeping obligations. Your phone number is retained only while your account is active and is deleted upon account deletion.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your account data, or close your account, by contacting us at dineshsp2003@gmail.com. You can stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, including hashed password storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Karmine is not directed to, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
